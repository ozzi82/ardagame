GAME DESIGN DOCUMENT — REALMS UNBOUND

1. GAME OVERVIEW

Game Name: Realms Unbound

Game Type: 3D Open-World Fantasy Adventure / Exploration / Racing / Mission Game

Visual Style: GTA 5-style realistic 3D graphics, detailed environments, realistic lighting, high-quality textures, realistic vehicles, and cinematic animations.

Main Concept:

Create a massive, fully 3D open-world fantasy game containing seven completely different lands. Players can freely explore the world, drive cars, walk around cities, discover secret locations, complete missions, collect vehicles, earn money, purchase properties, upgrade their equipment, and unlock new areas.

The game must feel alive and constantly offer new things to discover.

The most important priorities are:

1. A genuinely 3D open world.
2. An enormous interactive world map.
3. Seven unique fantasy lands.
4. Free driving and walking.
5. Hundreds of missions and activities.
6. A satisfying progression and unlocking system.
7. Continuous exploration and new discoveries.
8. A detailed vehicle collection and customization system.

The player should always have something interesting to work towards, but should never be forced to play every day.

⸻

2. THE MAIN WORLD

The game takes place in one enormous connected world containing seven fantasy lands.

Each land must have its own:

* Environment and landscape.
* Cities and villages.
* Roads and driving routes.
* NPC characters.
* Missions and storylines.
* Vehicles and transportation.
* Shops and garages.
* Hidden locations.
* Collectibles.
* Challenges.
* Special rewards.
* Unique weather and atmosphere.

The seven lands should connect through roads, bridges, tunnels, oceans, mountain passes, and other travel routes.

Players should be able to travel between lands without feeling like they are entering completely separate games.

4. THE 3D WORLD MAP

The world map is one of the most important parts of the entire game.

It must be a REAL 3D interactive map, not a flat image.

Features:

* Fully 3D world.
* Seven clickable lands.
* Rotate the map.
* Zoom in and out.
* Move the camera around the world.
* View cities, roads, mountains, rivers and landmarks.
* See discovered and undiscovered locations.
* Mission markers.
* Racing markers.
* Garage markers.
* Shop markers.
* Secret location markers.

When a player clicks one of the seven lands:

1. The camera smoothly zooms towards that land.
2. The land becomes the focus.
3. Its discovered locations appear.
4. Missions and activities become selectable.
5. The player can choose a destination.
6. A navigation route appears.

The map must visually represent the actual game world. Locations shown on the map must correspond to real locations in the playable 3D environment.

Include a world discovery percentage showing how much of the entire world the player has explored.

18. FINAL GAMEPLAY VISION

The final result should feel like a combination of:

* GTA 5: open-world freedom, realistic cities, vehicles and exploration.
* Forza Horizon: driving, racing and vehicle customization.
* Fantasy RPGs: magical lands, quests, exploration, bosses and discoveries.
* Open-world adventure games: secrets, collectibles, progression and an evolving world.

However, Realms Unbound should have its own identity.

The seven lands should feel connected, alive and worth exploring.

Players should be able to spend time driving through beautiful landscapes, discover a hidden cave, find a rare vehicle, complete a mission, purchase a house, upgrade their garage, and then discover an entirely new part of the world.

THE MOST IMPORTANT REQUIREMENT:

This must be a real, fully playable 3D open-world game, not a 2D map, a collection of screens, a website pretending to be a game, or a collection of static 3D images.

The ultimate goal is to create a massive, immersive fantasy world where exploration, freedom, missions, vehicles and continuous progression are equally important.
